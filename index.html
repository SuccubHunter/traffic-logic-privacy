Traffic Logic Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

1. Overview

Traffic Logic works without registration and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or access to your files.

This policy applies to the Traffic Logic: Route Puzzle game for Android, application ID com.succubhuntersinc.trafficlogic.game. The developer of the game is succubhuntersinc (“we”).

2. What data is processed

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. Only technical data about game stability leaves it.

DataWhat it includesWhere it is kept
ProgressCompleted levelsOn your device
SettingsLanguage, volume, mute, reduced motionOn your device
Technical dataCrash and error logs, information about freezes, performance and app configurationOn Unity servers
Installation IDA unique ID of this installation of the gameOn Unity servers

When technical data is sent, the Unity server receives the IP address of your connection. This is required for network communication.

If Android backup is turned on, the system may save your progress and settings to your Google account. We have no access to these copies.

The game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files or payment details. It has no ads, in-app purchases, accounts or chats, and it does not request an advertising ID.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We use technical data only to find and fix errors in the game. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

PurposeDataBasis
Saving progress and settings between sessionsProgress, settingsOperation of the game on your device; we do not receive this data
Finding and fixing crashes, errors and freezesTechnical data, installation IDLegitimate interest in keeping the game stable (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and similar rules in other countries)

4. Who receives the data

We do not sell data. Only Unity receives technical data, and Google Play distributes the game.

ServiceRoleDetails
Unity TechnologiesProcesses technical data and the installation ID on our behalfUnity Diagnostics privacy
Google PlayDistributes the game and processes installation data under its own rulesGoogle Privacy Policy

These services may process data outside your country. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

5. Retention, security and deletion

By default, Unity keeps personal data from crash reports for 90 days. We have no servers of our own with player data.

  • On your device. Progress and settings stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. After that, they can only be restored from a backup of your device.
  • Backups. They are managed by Android settings and your Google account.
  • Transfer. Unity encrypts technical data in transit.
  • Deletion on request. Write to us at the address in section 8. We do not have your name or contacts, so we may ask for details that help us find your installation. If we cannot find it, we will tell you.

6. Children

The game is not directed specifically at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe we have received a child’s data, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on your country, you may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, and object to processing.

The game has no separate switch for turning off crash reports. To exercise your rights, write to us. You may also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

8. Changes and contact

Send privacy questions to succubhuntersinc@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
